How this works (and its limits)

Real 15-minute and 1-hour stock bars from Alpaca (IEX feed) go through the bot's own setup finder and council. Option prices are modeled with Black-Scholes using each stock's last 20 days of movement ×1.15, plus a 4–7% bid/ask spread, then walked to the exit with the bot's real rules (stop −40%, target +60%, early profit at +30%, trailing stop, 3:40 PM / day-before-expiry exits, 2 trades at once, $62 per-trade budget). Real options get pricier before earnings and big events, and real fills can be worse, so treat these numbers as a best case. 6846 setups seen, 5321 had no affordable contract.
